01 / INTRODUCTION

Hydration, tuned to you.

NEPTUNE aims to make drinking water easier to remember and more enjoyable. The Trident Smart Hydration Bottle combines customizable flavor capsules, a smart display, basic water monitoring, and a clean reusable design around a simple daily habit.

Flavor CapsulesCustomize the taste of your water with your preferred flavor.
Smart DisplayAccess time, approximate temperature, and estimated water level.
Hydration AppSet goals, receive reminders, track progress, and maintain streaks.
Reusable DesignA single bottle built around everyday hydration.
02 / HOW TO USE

Four simple steps.

Trident keeps its core functions simple, so the smart features stay out of the way of the most important thing — drinking water.

01

Add a Capsule

Place your selected flavor capsule into the dedicated compartment in the bottle cap.

02

Single Tap

Tap the cap once to display the current time on the integrated digital display.

03

Double Tap

Tap twice to view the approximate water temperature and estimated remaining water level.

04

Use the App

Set a daily goal, receive reminders, monitor progress, and maintain your hydration streak.

03 / PRODUCT COMPONENTS

Designed around the Trident cap.

The cap brings together the flavor capsule compartment and smart display in one compact system. Open the cap, place the capsule in its dedicated compartment, and close it to use the bottle.
